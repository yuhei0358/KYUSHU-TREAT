よくある質問

FAQ

When do the first boxes ship?

We're confirming makers and shipping routes now and plan to send the first boxes in 2027. Waitlist members hear first and get founding prices.

Which countries do you ship to?

We're starting with the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia and the European Union. Tell us your country on the waitlist and we'll open where demand is.

Will I pay import duties?

Some countries charge duties or taxes on food from Japan. For the United States, duties now apply to most parcels from Japan. We'll show the full cost at checkout, including any duties we can prepay, so there are no surprises at the door.

Are the snacks vegan or allergy-friendly?

Most items are plant-based, and each one is labeled in English with its full ingredients and allergens. Some months may include wheat, soy, sesame or nuts. If you have a severe allergy, please check the booklet before eating.

Can I send a box as a gift?

Yes. Pick any plan, add the recipient's address, and we'll include a handwritten card.

Who is behind Kyushu Treat?

We're JourneyMate, a small team of private tour guides based in Fukuoka. Kyushu Treat is how we share the island with people who haven't made it here yet.
